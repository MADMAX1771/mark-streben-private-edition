04 / THE ARTIST

Mark
Streben.

Working across expressive abstraction and sculptural forms, Mark Streben creates compositions where technical imagination meets intuitive mark-making. His recurring atomic and tubular vocabulary suggests an artistic universe in constant transformation.

This private presentation offers a focused introduction to selected works. Further documentation, artwork specifications and provenance information may be requested through the representing team.
