02 / THE ARTISTIC UNIVERSE

The Atomic Language

A speculative visual language imagined at the threshold of the first light — where matter, energy and meaning have not yet separated.

BEFORE WORDS, THERE WAS ENERGY

Born of the
first collision.

Imagine the instant after the Big Bang: no alphabet, no grammar, no familiar civilisation. Only vibration, expansion, orbit, rupture and the beginnings of form. The Atomic Language is our curatorial name for the visual vocabulary running through Streben’s work.

Its marks suggest a message from somewhere beyond human history — part astronomical notation, part ceremonial glyph, part futuristic schematic. It is not a literal extraterrestrial language, but an artistic fiction: a system that looks as though it might have existed before speech.

A VISUAL GRAMMAR

◉

Origin

Concentric circles: the point of emergence, gravity and return.

⌁

Frequency

Repeated lines and oscillations: a signal without a known sender.

△

Structure

Triangulations and architectural fragments: matter finding its geometry.

✳

Expansion

Radiating intersections: energy moving beyond the frame.

THE UNTRANSLATABLE

Not written
to be read.
Made to be felt.

There is an atmosphere of science fiction and lunar archaeology here — the feeling of deciphering a lost code on the dark side of the Moon. Yet the work remains wholly its own: abstract painting, not an imitation of any film or fictional script.
