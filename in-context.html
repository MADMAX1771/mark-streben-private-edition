03 / ART & SPACE

In Context

An artwork does not end at its edges. Its scale, energy and presence change the architecture around it.

Art is not decoration.
It is an encounter.

In Context explores how Streben’s paintings and sculptural forms converse with the spaces they occupy. The intention is not simply to match a work to an interior, but to examine what happens when the two become part of the same visual experience.

01 / PUBLIC SPACE

Gallery scale.

In a generous architectural volume, a work becomes an event. Distance, sightlines and movement invite visitors to encounter it from multiple perspectives.

02 / PRIVATE SPACE

Living with art.

Within a private residence, an artwork changes over time. Light, daily rituals and proximity reveal details that a single viewing cannot.

03 / OBJECT & ARCHITECTURE

Beyond the canvas.

Sculptural forms bring the Atomic vocabulary into three dimensions, creating a dialogue between surface, mass and the surrounding environment.

These images are used as visual presentations. Locations, exhibition histories and actual placements require separate verification.
